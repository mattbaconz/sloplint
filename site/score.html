Score a page

Paste a URL, get its Slop Score.

sloplint reads the page's HTML and its stylesheets and grades them with the same engine as the CLI: the rules run in your browser, and the score card is yours to share.

A small fetcher reads the page as a logged-out visitor: the HTML, plus up to 8 stylesheets from the same site. It sends no cookies, honors robots.txt and stops at 2 MB. Pages that render with JavaScript, or sit behind a sign-in, come back empty or wrong here: use the bookmarklet on the live page instead.

      Sites with a strict Content-Security-Policy (GitHub, many banks and admin consoles) can block bookmarklets from loading outside code. Stylesheets from other origins that are served without CORS cannot be read by any script, so the overlay counts them instead of grading them.
