Technical Architecture

Engineered for agency cash flow anomalies

Generic invoicing tools assume clients type clean reference numbers into clean payment portals. Agency clients do not. Ledgerly resolves messy, real-world bank transfers through deterministic accounting rules.

Algorithmic remittance parser with fuzzy entity mapping

Corporate clients frequently remit funds under legal parent names, operating entities, or third-party payroll services. Ledgerly links bank transaction strings to your client directory using clearing identifiers, past transaction history, and exact amount verification.

DECOMPOSITION

Multi-invoice split payment allocation

When clients combine milestone invoices into one wire, Ledgerly decomposes line totals and applies funds across open accounts without leaving unallocated ledger fragments.

TOLERANCE

Intermediary bank fee auto-adjustment

International transfers often arrive $15 to $35 short due to correspondent routing fees. Configurable threshold rules mark invoices settled while recording wire deductions to expense accounts.

INTEGRATIONS

Two-way accounting ledger synchronization

Ledgerly writes payment records directly to QuickBooks Online, Xero, and FreshBooks in real time. Your accounting periods stay locked and reconciled without duplicate data entry.

PROTOCOL

Direct bank feed infrastructure

Read-only bank connections through Mercury, Chase Commercial, Silicon Valley Bank, and Brex refresh every 60 seconds with 256-bit TLS encryption.
