Matching that explains itself

Payment matching

Ledgerly scores each deposit against open invoices using the amount, the payer name, the remittance reference and your client's past payment habits. Scores above 90 close the invoice. Anything lower lands in a review queue with the reason shown beside it, such as "$100 short" or "two invoices total this amount".

1Deposit arrives from your bank feed, usually within 15 minutes.
2Ledgerly scores it against every open invoice for that client.
3High scores close the invoice and email a receipt. Low scores wait for you.

Partial and split payments

Apply one deposit across several invoices, or several deposits to one invoice, with the balance tracked.

Bank feeds

Connects to 11,000 banks through Plaid and GoCardless, read-only.

Automatic reminders

Sends a nudge 3 days before and 7 days after the due date, only to unpaid invoices.

Export to your books

Pushes matched payments to QuickBooks Online and Xero every night.
