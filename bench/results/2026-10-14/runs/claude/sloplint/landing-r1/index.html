How Ledgerly closes the loop

Matching that reads the transfer, not just the amount

Ledgerly compares the payer name, reference text, amount and date against every open invoice. When a client pays two invoices in one transfer or rounds off a bank fee, it still lands on the right ones. Anything below your confidence threshold goes to a review queue instead of being guessed.
