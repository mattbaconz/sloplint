Features

Everything between “sent” and “paid”

Built for teams of 2 to 30 who bill by project, retainer, or hour, and don’t have a finance department.

Automatic payment matching

Reference numbers, amounts, and payer names are matched to open invoices as the money arrives, including partial and combined payments.

Live bank feeds

Secure, read-only connections to thousands of banks. Your invoice list always reflects what has actually landed.
