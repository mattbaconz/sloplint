Features

Everything between "sent" and "paid"

Built for agencies of 2 to 50 people who bill by project, retainer and hour, and who'd rather not live in their bank portal.

Automatic payment matching

Amounts, references and payer names are compared against open invoices. Clear matches are applied instantly; edge cases land in a short review queue.

Read-only bank connections

Connect through secure open banking. Ledgerly can see transactions, never move money, and you can disconnect any time.

Polite reminders that work

Schedule friendly nudges before and after the due date. They stop the moment the matching payment arrives.

Clean, branded invoices

Send professional invoices with your logo, tax details and a pay-now link, in the currency your client expects.

Cash-flow at a glance

See what's outstanding, what's overdue and what's landing this week, without building a single pivot table.

Built for your accountant

Export reconciled ledgers to Xero, QuickBooks or CSV. Month-end stops being a scramble.
