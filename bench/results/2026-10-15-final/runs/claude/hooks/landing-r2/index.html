Reconciliation without the spreadsheet

Matching comes first. The rest of the invoicing tool exists to make matching accurate.

  1. 01

    Automatic payment matching

    Each payment is compared with open invoices by amount, payer name and the reference your client typed. Exact matches are marked paid without a click.

  2. 02

    Split and combined payments

    When a client pays two invoices in one transfer, or one invoice in two parts, Ledgerly proposes the allocation and waits for your approval.

  3. 03

    A review queue for the rest

    Unplaced payments land in one list with the closest invoice candidates beside each, so a human decision takes seconds.

  4. 04

    Invoices and reminders

    Send invoices by email or link, and schedule reminders that stop the moment the matching payment arrives.
