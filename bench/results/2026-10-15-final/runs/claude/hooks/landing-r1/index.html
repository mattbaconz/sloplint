What Ledgerly does, ranked by how much time it saves

  1. Automatic payment matching

    Each payment is compared with open invoices by amount, payer name and the reference text. A single clear match is applied and the invoice is marked paid.

  2. A short exception queue

    When a payment could settle two invoices, or covers part of one, it waits for you with the candidates listed. You choose once; Ledgerly remembers the payer.

  3. Reminders that stop when money arrives

    Overdue reminders go out on your schedule and are cancelled the moment the matching payment lands, so no client is chased for an invoice they paid.

  4. Plain exports for your accountant

    Download paid and open invoices as CSV, with the bank transaction ID next to each one.
