Features

Matching first, everything else second.

Features are listed in the order they save you time.

  1. 01 Automatic matching

    Payments find their invoices

    Ledgerly compares amount, payer name, and the reference your client typed against your open invoices, then closes the ones that agree.

  2. 02 Partial and combined payments

    One transfer, several invoices

    When a client pays two invoices in one transfer, or pays half now and half later, Ledgerly splits the amount and keeps the balance open.

  3. 03 Review queue

    Unclear payments wait for you

    Anything below the match threshold goes to a queue with the likely invoices ranked. Nothing is marked paid on a guess.

  4. 04 Reminders

    Overdue invoices chase themselves

    Reminders stop the moment a matching payment lands, so a client never gets a nudge for an invoice already settled.

  5. 05 Export

    Hand your accountant a clean ledger

    Download invoices and matched payments as CSV for any month.
