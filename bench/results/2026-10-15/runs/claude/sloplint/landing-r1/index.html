What Ledgerly does with each payment

  • Automatic matching

    Compares amount, payer name and the invoice number in the bank reference, then marks the invoice paid when one open invoice fits.

  • Split and combined payments

    One transfer that covers two invoices, or two transfers that cover one, are grouped and shown together for a single confirmation.

  • Review queue

    Payments with no confident match wait in a list with the closest open invoices beside them. Confirm with one click or assign by hand.

  • Payment reminders

    Overdue invoices send reminders on a schedule you set, and stop the moment the matching payment lands.

  • Bank-feed connection

    Read-only access to your business account. Ledgerly can see transactions; it cannot move money.
