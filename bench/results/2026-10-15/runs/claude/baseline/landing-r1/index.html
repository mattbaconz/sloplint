Features

Everything between “sent” and “settled.”

Invoicing is the easy part. Ledgerly handles the part nobody enjoys: figuring out who paid what.

Matches by reference, amount, payer and history. Handles partial, combined and overpaid payments without a fuss.

Live bank connections

Read-only bank feeds update through the day. We never move money and can’t initiate payments.

Gentle reminders

Retainers & recurring
