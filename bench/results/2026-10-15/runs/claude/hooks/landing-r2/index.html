What Ledgerly does with each payment

01

Automatic matching

Each incoming payment is compared with your open invoices by amount, payer name and payment reference. Exact matches close the invoice without a click.

02

Partial and combined payments

A client pays two invoices in one transfer, or half of one. Ledgerly splits the payment, leaves the remainder open, and shows the difference.

03

Review queue for the rest

Anything uncertain lands in one list with the candidate invoices beside it. You confirm or reassign; Ledgerly remembers the payer next time.

04

Overdue reminders

Invoices still open after their due date get a reminder email. Paid invoices stop reminders immediately because matching has already run.
