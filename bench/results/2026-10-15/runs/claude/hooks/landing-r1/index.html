What Ledgerly does, in order of how much time it saves

  1. 01

    Automatic payment matching

    Each incoming bank transaction is compared with your open invoices by amount, invoice number in the reference, and client name. A confident match closes the invoice without you touching it.

  2. 02

    Partial and combined payments

    A client pays half now and half later, or settles three invoices in one transfer. Ledgerly splits the payment across invoices and shows the remaining balance on each.

  3. 03

    A review queue for what it cannot match

    Unmatched payments land in one list with the closest candidate invoices beside them. Confirm with one click; Ledgerly uses your choice for the next payment from that client.

  4. 04

    Reminders that stop when the payment arrives

    Overdue reminders are sent by email on the schedule you set, and cancelled the moment the matching payment lands, so a client who has paid never gets chased.
