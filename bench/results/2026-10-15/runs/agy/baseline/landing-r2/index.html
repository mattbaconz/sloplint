Answers to common agency questions

Everything you need to know about bank safety, accounting sync, and automated matching.

Ledgerly’s neural matching engine evaluates multiple attributes: the exact dollar amount, the sender legal entity name, past remittance patterns, pending deliverable milestone dates, and active contract amounts. When confidence exceeds 98%, it auto-reconciles. For edge cases, Ledgerly presents a 1-click suggested match in your daily morning digest.
Never. Ledgerly uses strictly read-only banking API tokens through regulated partners like Plaid and direct bank Open Banking standards. Ledgerly cannot move funds, create wires, or modify bank account balances. Your banking credentials are encrypted with 256-bit AES encryption.
Ledgerly natively supports deposit and partial milestone billing. When a 50% deposit lands, Ledgerly recognizes the deposit term on the master agreement or invoice, applies the payment, updates the open balance, and attaches the partial payment proof to the client ledger in Xero or QuickBooks.
Intermediary wire fees (typically $15 to $35) are the number one cause of broken automatic matches in traditional software. Ledgerly lets you define tolerance ranges. When a client pays an invoice in full but $25 is shaved by routing banks, Ledgerly marks the invoice paid and books the variance directly to your bank fee expense account.
