The Agency Problem

Manual reconciliation is burning your agency’s billable hours.

Small agencies average 14 hours every month cross-referencing wires, client reference numbers, and Stripe payouts against accounting spreadsheets.

The Manual Spreadsheet Way

Logging into 4 bank portals every Friday afternoon to chase remittances.

  • ✕ Awkward client nudges: You email a partner asking for $30,000, only for them to reply: "We wired that 3 days ago, check your bank."
  • ✕ Cryptic wire memos: Client accounting teams input abbreviated project codes or legal entity names that don't match invoice titles.
  • ✕ Stripe & FX fee mismatches: A $10,000 international invoice lands as $9,762.40 after wire deductions, leaving open balance discrepancies.
  • ✕ Double entry in accounting: Re-keying transactions into QuickBooks or Xero leads to duplicate customer records and phantom invoices.
The Ledgerly Auto-Reconciliation Way

Zero-touch verification. Payments match and clear within seconds of arriving.

  • ✓ Self-silencing reminders: Pending overdue notifications automatically cancel the microsecond payment touches your bank feed.
  • ✓ Fuzzy matching engine: Ledgerly connects parent company EINs, subsidiary names, wire codes, and invoice numbers seamlessly.
  • ✓ Automatic fee isolation: Intermediary bank fees, Stripe processing percentages, and FX spreads are isolated and booked to fee accounts.
  • ✓ Instant bi-directional sync: Invoices mark as paid in Xero, QuickBooks, and FreshBooks in real time with zero manual intervention.
