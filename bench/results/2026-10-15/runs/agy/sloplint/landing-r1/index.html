02 / RECONCILIATION ENGINE

Three-tier resolution for messy client references

Accounts payable teams frequently truncate agency invoice numbers, append internal purchase orders, or pay multiple milestones in single lump-sum transfers. Ledgerly applies three successive resolution tiers to clear open balances without manual cross-checking.

Ranked matching pipeline

When transactions land from your Open Banking feed, the matching engine tests candidate records against three deterministic gates:

  1. 01
    Deterministic Reference Extraction

    The parser normalizes incoming reference strings by removing punctuation, ignoring bank routing prefixes, and matching alphanumeric invoice numbers directly against open receivables.

  2. 02
    Counterparty Account & Net Balance Verification

    If the client entered a blank or internal purchase order reference, Ledgerly verifies the sending IBAN or sort code against known client bank details and checks for exact net invoice totals.

  3. 03
    Split & Combined Payment Allocation

    When a client combines a project milestone with an ongoing monthly retainer in one transfer, the system matches the total against combined open balances and marks both invoices paid with an linked audit record.
