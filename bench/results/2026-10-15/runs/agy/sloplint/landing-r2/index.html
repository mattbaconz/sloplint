Reconciliation features ranked by agency operational impact

  1. 01 Multi-bank feed ingestion

    Connects directly to Chase, Silicon Valley Bank, Mercury, Brex, and 11,000 regional institutions via Plaid Exchange and open banking APIs. Deposits synchronize automatically every 15 minutes without manual CSV exports.

  2. 02 Remittance parser and fuzzy matching

    Decodes truncated bank memos, sender legal entity variants, purchase order numbers, and invoice identifiers. Matches incoming funds to open receivables across net-15, net-30, and milestone contracts.

  3. 03 Intermediary wire fee detection

    Identifies when a client wire arrives short by the standard $15 to $35 correspondent banking deduction. Automatically books the difference to your bank fee expense account and marks the invoice paid in full.

  4. 04 Bi-directional ledger sync

    Writes reconciled payment records directly to QuickBooks Online and Xero. Attaches the bank clearance timestamp and transaction ID to the invoice audit log for month-end reconciliation.

  5. 05 Split remittances and retainer balances

    Handles single payments covering multiple project invoices or monthly retainer advances. Distributes line-item credits across open billing items and updates client retainer drawdowns in real time.
