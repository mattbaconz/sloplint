One room. One press. One label.

Offset Press is a print studio and record label run from a single workspace. The same people pull the screens, cut the lacquers' artwork and pack the orders.

We make things in runs small enough to finish by hand. When an edition is gone, the screens are cleaned and the run is closed.

Never reissued
  1. 1SeparateArtwork split into one layer per ink.
  2. 2PullEach colour squeegeed through its screen, one sheet at a time.
  3. 3PressRecords cut, plated and pressed at an outside plant.
  4. 4NumberEvery piece signed, numbered and packed flat.
