How preorders work

  1. Choose by Thursday night

    Fill in the form below with what you want and which morning you will collect. Orders close Thursday at 8 p.m. so we can mix dough Friday.1

  2. We confirm by email

    You get a reply from us with your order and pickup window. If we are out of something, we tell you then, not at the counter.

  3. Pick up and pay at the counter

    Come between 8 a.m. and noon with your name. Your bag is labeled and waiting. We take cash and cards.

A loaf every weekend, without ordering

Pick a size once and we set the bread aside every Saturday. Skip any week by telling us before Thursday. Cancel by replying to any of our emails.
