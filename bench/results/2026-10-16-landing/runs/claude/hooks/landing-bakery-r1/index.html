How preorders work

Four steps, no account

  1. 01

    Pick your bake

    Choose loaves and pastries from the menu above. Quantities are capped so everyone gets a turn.

  2. 02

    Send the form

    Orders close Thursday at 8 pm. We email a confirmation with your pickup window within a few hours.

  3. 03

    We bake Friday night

    Dough goes in after close on Friday, and the first loaves come out around 5 am.

  4. 04

    Pick up and pay

    Saturday 8 am to noon, Sunday 8 to 11 am. Your bag is on the shelf by the door. Card or cash.

Start your preorder Bags are held until 1 pm on the day.
