One studio. Two presses.

Offset Press is a screen-print shop and a record label under one roof. The same people who pull the posters sleeve the records.

Every poster is printed by hand, one color at a time, on archival cotton stock. Every record is cut, pressed and checked before it ships. When an edition is gone, the screens are cleaned and the run is over. No reprints.

We work with bands, illustrators and venues. If you make something that deserves a short, numbered run, write to us through the drop list.

Largest run we press 500 Copies, on the 7 in. Posters stop at 250. Then the edition is closed.
PostersWater-based inks, one screen per color, numbered in pencil.
Records12 in LPs and EPs, 7 in singles. Printed sleeves, inner bags included.
ShippingPosters ship flat in rigid mailers. Records ship in stiffened boxes.
