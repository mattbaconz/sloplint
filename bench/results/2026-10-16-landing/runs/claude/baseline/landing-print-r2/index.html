About the studio

Two presses, one room.

Offset Press started in 2014 as a garage screen-printing table making gig posters for friends' bands. Those bands kept asking whether we could do the records too, so we started a label.

Today the studio is a six-person team. Every poster is pulled by hand, one colour at a time, on archival cotton stock with water-based inks. Every record is mastered with the artist and pressed in short runs, with sleeves printed in-house.

We keep runs small on purpose. It keeps the work personal and it keeps the artists paid fairly: 50% of every record's profit goes straight to the band.
