About the studio

Two presses.
One small crew.

Offset Press started in a rented garage with a single-carousel press and a stack of band flyers. Today we're a print studio and record label under one roof, working directly with musicians and illustrators on posters, sleeves and records.

We mix our own inks, pull every sheet by hand and number every edition. No reprints, no mass production, no shortcuts.
