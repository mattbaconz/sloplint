Alerts for slow, not just down

Page on slow, before it is down

Set a threshold per endpoint and a count of consecutive slow checks. Pingwell calls the person on the schedule, not a shared inbox. One slow response is logged; three in a row is a page.

$ pingwell monitor add https://api.example.com/v1/orders \
  --interval 30s --latency-over 400ms --consecutive 3 \
  --page schedule:backend-oncall

Incoming call: Pingwell

"backend-oncall. GET slash v1 slash orders is answering in 605 milliseconds, over your 400 millisecond limit. Press 1 to acknowledge."

  • On-call schedules and escalation

    Weekly rotations, overrides for sick days, and a second engineer paged if the first does not acknowledge within the time you choose.

  • Checks from 6 regions

    A page fires only when at least two regions agree, so a bad route in one place does not wake anyone.

  • Phone, SMS and Slack

    Voice calls for pages, Slack for the thread that follows, SMS as the fallback when the call goes to voicemail.

  • Latency history per endpoint

    p50, p95 and p99 for each monitor, kept for 13 months on Team, so you can see when the slowdown started.
