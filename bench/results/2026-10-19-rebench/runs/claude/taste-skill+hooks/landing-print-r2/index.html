One press. Two trades.

Offset Press is a print studio and a record label sharing one room. The same hands that pull a poster squeegee across a screen cut the sleeve for the record that goes with it.

We keep runs at 100 so every copy gets attention. Inks are mixed by hand, screens are exposed in the studio, and no release is reprinted. When the last copy ships, the screens are cleaned and the artwork is retired.

We work with musicians and illustrators who want a small, honest release instead of a large, forgettable one.

  1. ExposeArtwork is burned onto a fresh screen, one per colour.
  2. PullEach sheet is printed by hand, black first, blue second.
  3. NumberEvery copy is signed and numbered out of 100.
