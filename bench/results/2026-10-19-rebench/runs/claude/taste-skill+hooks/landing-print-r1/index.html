One press. One small room.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. We pull every poster by hand and press every record in short runs.

Posters
Screen-printed by hand, two to four colors
Records
12″ and 7″ vinyl, 180 g
Run size
100 to 300 copies, then it is gone
Reprints
None. A sold-out run stays sold out

The name is the technique. When two screens are slightly out of line, the colors shift, and that shift is the mark of a hand-pulled print. We keep it on purpose.

The label side releases the music we want to hold in our hands. Every record ships with a print from the same artwork, pulled in the studio.
