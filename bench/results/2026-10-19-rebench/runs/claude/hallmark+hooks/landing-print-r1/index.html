One room. Two presses.

Offset Press started as a screen-print bench and grew a record label because the bands we printed for kept asking who could press their album. Now the same room does both.

We pull every sheet by hand, one color at a time, and cap each run so a number on the print means something. Records come back from the plant and get sleeved, stamped and numbered at the same bench.
