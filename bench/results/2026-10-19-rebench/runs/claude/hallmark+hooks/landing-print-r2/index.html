The studio

One press, one label, one small team. We print the posters for the bands we press, and press the records for the artists we print for.

Screen printing is slow on purpose. Each color is its own stencil and its own pass, so every sheet is touched many times before it leaves the rack.

We sell direct. No reprints, no variants, no restocks once a run is gone.

  1. DrawArtwork is separated into one layer per ink.
  2. BurnEach layer is exposed onto its own mesh screen.
  3. PullInk is dragged through the screen by hand, one sheet at a time.
  4. PressRecords go to the plant, sleeves come back to us for printing.
