One room. One squeegee. One label.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. We pull every poster by hand on a screen press, one ink at a time, and we release the records of the bands we print for.

The two halves feed each other. A record gets a poster; a poster gets a soundtrack. Runs stay small so each copy stays good.
