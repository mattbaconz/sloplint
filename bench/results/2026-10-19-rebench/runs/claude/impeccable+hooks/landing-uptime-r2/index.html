What happens between a slow response and a ringing phone

  • Latency thresholds

    Set a p95 limit per endpoint in milliseconds. An alert needs 3 consecutive probes over the limit, so one slow request does not wake anyone.

  • Paging that rings

    Phone call, SMS or Slack message to whoever is on the schedule. The call repeats every 2 minutes until someone presses 1 to acknowledge.

  • Escalation

    Unacknowledged after 5 minutes? The next engineer on the list gets paged. Chain as many steps as your team has people.

  • Rotations

    Weekly or daily on-call schedules with overrides for swaps. Import your current rota as a .ics file.

  • Probes from 6 regions

    Frankfurt, Virginia, Oregon, Singapore, São Paulo and Sydney. A region-only slowdown shows as a region, not as an outage.

  • Config in your repo

    Define monitors in a pingwell.yml file and apply it from CI, or use the REST API and Terraform provider.
