One room. Two presses.

Offset Press is a print studio and record label run from a single workshop. The same people who pull the screens master the records and fold the sleeves.

We print with water-based inks on uncoated stock, and we work with bands and illustrators we would pay to see. Nothing is reprinted, restocked or discounted.

  1. 1
    DrawOne layer per color, on film.
  2. 2
    PullInk squeegeed by hand, one sheet at a time.
  3. 3
    PressRecords cut, pressed and sleeved in house.
  4. 4
    NumberSigned in pencil. Then the screen is reclaimed.
