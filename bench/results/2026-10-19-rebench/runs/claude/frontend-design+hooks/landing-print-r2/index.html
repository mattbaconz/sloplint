The studio

Offset Press is one room with a press, a drying rack and a record shelf. We print gig posters for bands we like, then press records for the same bands.

Each color is its own screen, pulled by hand and aligned by eye. Small shifts between layers stay in. That is how you can tell a screen print from a copy of one.

We keep runs short on purpose. Nothing is reprinted, and every sheet and sleeve carries its edition number in pencil.

Posters
Water-based inks on cotton and uncoated stock
Records
12-inch and 7-inch vinyl, pressed at a partner plant
Sleeves
Printed in-house, folded and numbered by hand
Shipping
Posters flat in rigid mailers, records in stiff cartons
