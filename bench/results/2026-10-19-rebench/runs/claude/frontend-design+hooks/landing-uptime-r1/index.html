Built around one decision: who gets woken up

Alert on latency, not only on downtime

Set a p95 limit per endpoint and how many consecutive checks must breach it. A single slow response from one region does not page anyone.

monitor: POST /v1/checkout
p95_limit: 800ms
breach_after: 3 consecutive checks
regions: [fra, iad, sin, syd, gru]
page: oncall.payments

Rotations that match how your team actually works

Weekly handoffs, follow-the-sun shifts and overrides for a sick day. If the first engineer does not acknowledge, the next one is called.

  • 00:00Call on-call engineer
  • 00:05No answer: SMS + call secondary
  • 00:15No answer: call team lead
  • Any timeAcknowledge by keypad or reply

Five check regions

Frankfurt, Virginia, Singapore, Sydney and São Paulo. An alert needs agreement from at least two.

Incident timeline
