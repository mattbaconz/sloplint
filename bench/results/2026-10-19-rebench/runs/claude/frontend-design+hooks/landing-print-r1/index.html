One shop. Two trades.

We print the posters and press the records.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. Every poster is pulled by hand on our own screens, one ink at a time. Every record gets a sleeve printed in the same room.

We keep runs small on purpose. When an edition sells out, the screens are cleaned and the design is retired.
