What happens between a slow response and a ringing phone

  • Thresholds per endpoint

    Set a p95 limit on each route. POST /v1/charges can page at 400 ms while GET /v1/search waits until 500 ms.

    p95 > N ms

  • Phone call, then escalation

    The on-call engineer gets a call and an SMS. If nobody acknowledges within 5 minutes, Pingwell moves to the next person in the rotation.

    ack in 5 min

  • Two regions must agree

    A single slow probe does not wake anyone up. An alert fires only when at least two of six regions see the breach.

    2 of 6

  • Rotations you already keep

    Import the schedule from an iCalendar feed, so the person who gets paged is the person your calendar says is on call.

    .ics import 
# pingwell.yml
monitors:
  - url: https://api.example.com/v1/charges
    method: POST
    interval: 30s
    p95_over: 400ms
    consecutive: 3
    regions_agree: 2
    page: payments-oncall
