Press and label

Offset Press started with one screen and a borrowed squeegee. The poster side prints gig posters and art editions by hand, one colour at a time, on paper we cut ourselves.

The label side presses short runs of vinyl for bands we have printed for. The sleeve and the poster come off the same bench, so they match.

We never reprint a sold-out edition. The screen is cleaned, and the next job begins.

1–4ink colours per poster
40–300copies in a run
2presses: one flatbed, one record
0reprints of sold-out editions
