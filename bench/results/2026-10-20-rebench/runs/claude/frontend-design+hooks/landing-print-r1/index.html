One studio. Print and press.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. We pull every poster by hand on a screen bed in the studio, one color per pass, and release records from the bands we work with.

Runs stay small on purpose. When an edition sells out, the screens are reclaimed and the design is retired.

  1. Draw it separatedEach color gets its own film. A three-color poster means three screens.
  2. Pull by handWater-based ink, one squeegee pass per color, registered by eye against the marks.
  3. Number and signEvery sheet is numbered in pencil against the edition size.
  4. Press the recordSleeves carry the same print as the poster. Vinyl is pressed in runs of 250 to 500.
