One job: the right person hears about slow, fast.

Thresholds on p95, not on a single slow request

Set a limit per endpoint. Pingwell pages only after two consecutive checks from at least two regions are over it, so one slow hop in a single region does not wake anyone.

Each monitor is a short config file you keep in the repo.

monitor "POST /v1/checkout" {
  every     = "30s"
  regions   = ["fra", "iad", "sin", "syd", "gru"]
  p95_over  = "800ms"
  confirm   = 2
  escalate  = "primary, then secondary after 5m"
}

  • On-call schedules

    Weekly rotations, overrides for the swap you agreed in Slack, and a secondary who is paged if the primary has not acknowledged in 5 minutes.

  • Phone, SMS and push

    A call is the default for a breach. Warnings go to push. You choose per monitor.

  • Breach timeline

    Every page shows the region, the p95 at the time and who acknowledged, so the postmortem starts with facts.

  • Slack and webhooks

    Post to a channel on page, acknowledge and recovery. Webhooks send JSON to your own tools.
