One shop. Ink and wax.

Offset Press is a small print studio that also runs a record label. The same hands that pull the squeegee cut the jackets, so every record ships in a sleeve we printed ourselves.

We work in short runs on purpose. Each poster and record is numbered, and each edition is closed when the last copy is sold.

  1. DrawArtwork is separated into one layer per ink.
  2. BurnEach layer is exposed onto its own screen.
  3. PullInk is pulled through by hand, one color at a time.
  4. PressRecords are pressed at a partner plant and sleeved here.
