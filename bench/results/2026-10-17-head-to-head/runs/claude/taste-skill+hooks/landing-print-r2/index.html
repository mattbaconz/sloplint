Studio

One press. One label. No reprints.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. We pull every poster by hand, one color at a time, and press the records we release in short runs.

The same people design the sleeve, mix the sound and run the squeegee. That keeps the editions small and the prices honest.

  1. DrawArtwork is separated into one layer per ink.
  2. PullEach color is squeegeed through its own screen, then dried before the next.
  3. PressRecords are cut, pressed and sleeved in the same short run as the matching poster.
