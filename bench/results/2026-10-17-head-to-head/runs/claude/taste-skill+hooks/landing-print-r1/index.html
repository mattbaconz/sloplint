One studio. Ink and grooves.

We make the poster and the record in the same building, for the same bands.

Offset Press is an independent print studio and record label. We burn our own screens, mix our own inks and pull every sheet by hand, one color at a time.

The label side puts out records by artists we play with and print for. Each release gets a poster, a sleeve and a numbered run. When it is gone, it is gone.

  1. 1BurnArtwork is exposed onto a screen, one screen per color.
  2. 2PullInk goes through the screen by hand, sheet by sheet.
  3. 3NumberEach sheet and record is signed and numbered by edition.
  4. 4ShipPosters go out flat in a rigid mailer. Records go out in a stiff box.
