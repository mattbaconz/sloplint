Order by Thursday, pick up warm

We bake only what has been ordered, plus a small handful for walk-ins. That means less waste for us and a loaf with your name on it for you.

  1. Choose your bread and pastries

    Pick from the weekend menu above and set a quantity for each item.

  2. Send the form before Thursday 8pm

    We email you back within a day to confirm what is baking and your pickup window.

  3. Collect on Saturday or Sunday

    Walk in, say your name, and your bag is on the shelf behind the counter. You pay at pickup, by card or cash.
