Order by Thursday, pick up on the weekend

  1. Choose what you want

    Fill in the form below with quantities. You will get a confirmation email within a day.

  2. We bake to your count

    Dough is mixed Thursday night and baked Friday and Saturday. Nothing is baked without a name on it, except a small shelf for walk-ins.

  3. Pick up and pay at the window

    Give your name at the window. Cards and cash both work, and your bag is waiting.

Preorders close Thursday at 8 pm. Pickup is Saturday 8 to 12 and Sunday 8 to 11.
