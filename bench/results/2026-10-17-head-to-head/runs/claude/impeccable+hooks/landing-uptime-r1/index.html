One alert, aimed at one person.

Features, ranked
p95 > 400ms

Set a latency threshold per endpoint. Pingwell pages only after the breach holds for the number of consecutive checks you choose, so one slow request does not wake anyone.

# pingwell.yml
monitor: POST https://api.harborlane.dev/v1/checkout
interval: 30s
regions: [iad1, fra1, sin1, sfo1, syd1]
alert:
  p95_over: 400ms
  for_checks: 3
  page: oncall/payments

  • On-call routing

    Pages the engineer on the current rotation by phone, SMS or push. Unacknowledged after 5 minutes, it escalates to the next person.

  • Multi-region probes

    A breach counts only when at least 2 regions agree, which filters out one bad network path.

  • Config in your repo

    Monitors live in pingwell.yml and deploy with pingwell apply.

  • Latency history

    p50, p95 and p99 per endpoint, kept for 30 days on Team.
