The studio

One press room, one label. The same hands that pull the squeegee cut the lacquers and pack the sleeves.

We print posters for the bands we release, and for a few we only wish we did. Every run is capped before the first screen is burned, so the number on the corner means something.

Records are pressed at an outside plant and finished here: sleeves printed, inserts folded, copies checked on the turntable before they ship.

  1. BurnOne screen per color, exposed on site.
  2. PullInk drawn by hand, one sheet at a time.
  3. PressLacquers cut, vinyl pressed at a partner plant.
  4. NumberEvery piece signed and numbered in pencil.
