One press. One label.

Offset Press prints posters for the bands we release, and for other people's bands too. Every sheet is pulled through a hand-cut screen, one ink at a time.

The label side presses vinyl in small runs. Artwork, sleeve and record come from the same room, so the poster matches the record it belongs to.

  • ProcessScreen print, water-based ink
  • Records7 in and 12 in vinyl
  • NumberingSigned and numbered by hand
