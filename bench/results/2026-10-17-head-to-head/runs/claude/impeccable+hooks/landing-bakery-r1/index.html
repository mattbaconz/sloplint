Friday 17 October 2026 From the bench at Crumb & Rye

Dear neighbor,

We bake the weekend's bread on Friday night and Saturday before dawn, and we only bake what you have ordered. That is the whole idea behind Crumb & Rye: you tell us by Thursday, we shape the loaves that evening, and they come out of the oven a few hours before you pick them up.1

The sourdough starter is the same one we have fed every morning since we opened. The rye is milled coarse, the croissants are laminated by hand, and the cardamom buns sell out first, so order those early.

See you at the side door,
Crumb & Rye

How preorders work

  1. Mon to Thu

    Send your order

    Pick your loaves and pastries in the form below and choose Saturday or Sunday. It takes about a minute.

  2. Thu 8 pm

    We close the list

    You get an email that evening confirming what we are baking for you, and the total you will pay.

  3. Sat, Sun

    Collect at the side door

    Pickup is 8 am to noon. Give your name, take your bag, pay at the counter. The exact address is in your confirmation.
