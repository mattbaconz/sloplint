Built for the one person holding the pager.

Small teams don't need a wall of graphs. They need one alert that is right, sent to one person who can act.

  • Latency thresholds, per endpoint

    Set a limit on any URL, such as p95 > 500ms. Pingwell only fires when it holds across consecutive checks, so one slow blip stays quiet.

  • Pages the actual on-call

    Rotations decide who gets the page. If they don't acknowledge in time, it escalates to the next person.

  • Checks from outside your network

    Probes run from several regions, so you see what a customer sees, not what your load balancer thinks.

  • Phone, SMS, or chat

    Pick the channel per person. Push to the phone, text as a fallback, or post to your team's chat room.

  • Auth headers and request bodies

    Monitor real, authenticated endpoints, not only a public health route. Secrets are stored encrypted.

  • Recovery notices

    When latency drops back under the line, the incident closes itself and tells the channel, with how long it lasted.
