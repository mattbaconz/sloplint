Latency thresholds, not just up or down

An API that answers in six seconds is down to your users. Alert on p50, p95, or p99 against a limit you choose per endpoint.

Pages the on-call engineer

Sync your rotation once. Pingwell rings the person on shift by push, SMS, or phone call, and nobody else gets woken up.

on-call → push, then sms, then call

Escalation when no one answers

If the first page goes unacknowledged, it moves to the secondary, then to the team lead. You define the wait between each step.

no ack in 5m → escalate

Checks from outside your network

Probes run from multiple regions, so a slow route or a bad deploy in one place does not look like a global outage, and vice versa.
