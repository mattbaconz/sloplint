How preorders work

Order by Thursday. Pick up warm.

  1. Choose

    Pick your loaves and pastries from this weekend's menu. The menu refreshes every Monday.

  2. Reserve

    Send the form below by Thursday at 9 pm. We confirm your order by email.

  3. We bake

    Your order goes in the oven early Saturday or Sunday, so nothing sits around.

  4. Collect & pay

    Come by during your pickup window, give us your name, and pay at the counter.
