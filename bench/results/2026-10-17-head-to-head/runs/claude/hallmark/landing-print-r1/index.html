One room, two presses

Offset Press started as a print table in the back of a practice space. Bands wanted posters, then someone asked if we could do the sleeve too, and eventually the sleeve needed a record in it.

Now the studio runs both sides: a screenprint shop that pulls every sheet by hand, and a small label that works with a pressing plant to make records in runs of a few hundred.

We keep editions small on purpose. Every poster is numbered, every record is pressed once, and nothing gets a second run.

  1. 01

    Draw it in layers

    Each colour is its own separation, drawn or cut by hand so the overlaps do the mixing.

  2. 02

    Pull it by hand

    One squeegee, one sheet at a time, on cotton stock. Every pull is checked before it’s kept.

  3. 03

    Press it once

    Records are cut, plated, and pressed in a single small batch. We number the edition and retire the plates.
