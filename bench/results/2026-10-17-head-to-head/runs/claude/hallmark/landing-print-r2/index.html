A press and a label under one roof

Offset Press started as a print studio that kept getting asked to make record sleeves. Now the same room pulls the posters, cuts the sleeves, and puts out the records that go inside them.

We print on a manual carousel with water-based inks, one colour at a time, and register every sheet by eye. Nothing is reprinted. Each run is sized to what we can finish properly, then numbered and closed.

Posters
One to four colours, pulled by hand, numbered in pencil.
Records
Short pressing runs of artists we would buy ourselves.
Commissions
Gig posters and sleeves for bands, venues and festivals.
