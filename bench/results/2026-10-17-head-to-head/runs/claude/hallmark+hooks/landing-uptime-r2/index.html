01

Latency alerts that reach a person

01.1

Thresholds on p95, not just up or down

An endpoint that answers in 4 seconds is down for your users. Alert on the percentile that hurts.

# pingwell.yml
checks:
  - url: https://api.example.com/v1/orders
    every: 30s
    regions: [eu-west, us-east, ap-south]
    p95_over: 300ms
    for: 2 checks
    page: oncall-backend

01.4

Quiet hours and maintenance windows

Mute a check during a deploy. Pingwell resumes it on its own and pages if the endpoint is still slow.

$ pingwell mute orders-api --for 20m --reason "deploy 2026.10.17"
muted until 03:34 UTC, resumes automatically
