The studio

One shop. Two presses.

Offset Press prints posters for bands and presses records for the same bands. Art and audio come out of one room, so a sleeve is never an afterthought.

Every run is small on purpose. We number it, sign it, and stop.

  1. 01

    Separate

    Each color gets its own hand-cut or photo-exposed screen.

  2. 02

    Pull

    Ink goes through the mesh one color per pass, registered by eye and by mark.

  3. 03

    Press

    Records are pressed at an outside plant and packed in our printed sleeves.
