How preorders work

  1. Choose your bake

    Pick loaves and pastries from this weekend’s menu and tell us whether you want Saturday or Sunday.

  2. Send it by Thursday, 9pm

    Orders after the cutoff go on a waitlist. We bake extra when we can and will email you if there is a loaf for you.

  3. Get a confirmation

    We reply to your email with your pickup window and the total, so you know what to expect at the counter.

  4. Pick up and pay

    Your bag is at the counter with your name on it. Pay by card or cash when you collect.

A loaf waiting every weekend
