What Pingwell does, in order of use

  • Latency thresholds

    Set a p95 limit per endpoint in milliseconds. An alert opens after the limit is exceeded for the number of consecutive checks you choose (default 3).

  • Paging

    Phone call, SMS or push to whoever is on call. If they do not acknowledge within the window you set, it escalates to the next person.

  • On-call rotation

    Weekly or daily handoffs for teams of two or more. Overrides take effect immediately and appear in the incident log.

  • Multi-region checks

    Every monitor runs from us-east-1, eu-west-1 and ap-south-1. A page fires only when the threshold is crossed in at least two regions.

  • Monitors as code

    Create and update monitors with the REST API or a YAML file in your repo. Changes are versioned and show who edited a threshold.
