The studio

Offset Press is one room with a press, a drying rack and a record shelf. We print posters for bands and film nights, then press the same bands on vinyl.

Every poster is pulled by hand, one color at a time, on uncoated cotton stock. Every record gets a sleeve printed in the same room.

  • PrintWater-based inks on 100% cotton paper
  • PressRecords pressed at an independent plant, 180 g
  • RunsEdition sizes are printed on every release and never reopened
