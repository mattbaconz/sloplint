How a preorder works

  1. Pick your bread by Thursday, 8 pm

    Fill in the form below with what you want and which morning you will come. We read every order by hand.

  2. We confirm by email on Friday

    You get one message with your order, the total, and your pickup window. Anything we cannot bake, we tell you then.

  3. Collect it warm at the side door

    Give your name, take your bag, and pay at the counter by card or cash. Bags are held until 11 am.
