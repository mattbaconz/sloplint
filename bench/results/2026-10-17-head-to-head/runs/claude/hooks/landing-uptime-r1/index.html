01

Latency alerts that reach a person

Ranked by how often teams touch them: thresholds first, escalation second, history last.

  • Latency thresholds per endpoint

    Set a p95 limit and a window. Pingwell pages only when the window average stays over the limit, so one slow request does not wake anyone.

    window 1–60 min

  • Phone, SMS and push paging

    The on-call engineer is called first. If nobody acknowledges within your timeout, the next person on the rotation is called.

    ack timeout 2–30 min

  • Checks from 3 regions

    A failure must show in 2 of 3 regions before it pages, which filters out a bad network path.

    interval 30 s

  • Rotation schedule

    Weekly or daily handoffs with overrides for holidays and swaps.

    handoff 09:00 local 
pingwell monitor add https://api.example.com/v1/orders \
  --p95 800ms --window 5m \
  --regions 3 --quorum 2 \
  --page oncall
