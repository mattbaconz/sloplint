How preorders work

  1. Pick your loaves by Thursday, 6 pm

    We bake what is ordered plus a small walk-up batch, so the preorder list is the only sure way to get a loaf.

  2. We confirm by email on Friday

    You get your slip with a pickup time. Need to change a quantity? Reply to that email before 9 pm Friday.

  3. Collect at the side window

    Give your name, take your bag, pay by card or cash. Nora or one of the weekend bakers hands it over, still warm.
