How a preorder works

Every weekend we bake roughly what we've been asked for, plus a small tray for walk-ins. Ordering ahead is how you get the loaf you want, and it's how we keep the leftover bin nearly empty.

  1. MON to THU

    Choose your bread

    Pick from the weekend menu and tell us Saturday or Sunday. Order up to Thursday at 8 pm.

  2. THU night

    We confirm by email

    You get a short note with your order number and pickup window. Sold out? We say so and offer the closest swap.

  3. SAT, SUN

    Pick up warm, pay at the counter

    Come to the side window, give your name, and pay by card or cash. Nothing is charged online.

A weekly loaf, three sizes
