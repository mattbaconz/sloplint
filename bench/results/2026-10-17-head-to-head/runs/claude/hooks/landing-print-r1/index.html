The studio

One room. One press. Every sheet pulled by hand, every record sleeved by us.

Offset Press is a print studio and record label run by the same small crew. We pull posters on a manual four-colour press and press vinyl in short runs, so a release is never larger than the people who want it.

Editions are capped and numbered. When a run ends, the screens are reclaimed and the art is retired.

Process
Water-based screen printing
Stock
100 lb cotton and uncoated board
Records
7" and 12", short-run pressing
Numbering
Signed and numbered, by hand
