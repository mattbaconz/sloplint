The studio

One press. One room. Every sheet pulled by hand.

Offset Press prints posters and releases records for bands we would go see anyway.

Inks are mixed to order and each colour is a separate screen, so a three-colour poster is three passes through the same hands. The label side presses vinyl in short runs and ships the sleeve printed in the same studio.
