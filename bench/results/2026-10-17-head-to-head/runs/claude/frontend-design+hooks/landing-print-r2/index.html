Ink first. Then sound.

Offset Press is a print studio and record label under one roof. The same people who pull the posters press the records, so the sleeve, the poster and the music are designed together.

We print on manual presses, one color at a time, and we stop when the run is done.

  1. Draw the art and separate it into one screen per color.
  2. Pull each color by hand and let every layer dry before the next.
  3. Sign and number each sheet, then pack the run flat.
