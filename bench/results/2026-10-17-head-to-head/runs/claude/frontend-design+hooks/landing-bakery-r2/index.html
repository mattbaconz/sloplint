How preorders work

Four steps across one week, and you only do the first.

  1. Monday to Thursday 8 p.m.

    Send your order

    Fill in the form below with what you want and the day you will collect. We confirm by email within a few hours.

  2. Friday

    We mix and shape

    Your count goes on the bake sheet. Dough rests overnight in the cold, which is where the flavor comes from.

  3. Saturday or Sunday, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.

    Pick up at the counter

    Say your name at 214 Alder Street. Your bag is on the shelf behind the register, still warm.

  4. At the counter

    Pay when you collect

    Card or cash. If plans change, email us by Friday noon and we will drop the order without a charge.
