How preorders work

  1. Pick your bread and pastries

    Any time before Thursday 9 pm

    Choose quantities in the form below. You will see your total as you go.

  2. We bake to the orders

    Friday and Saturday

    Preorders are baked first, so what you ordered is set aside before the walk-in line starts.

  3. Collect it at the counter

    Saturday or Sunday, 8 to 11 am

    Give your name, pay at the counter, and take your bag. Loaves are still warm in the first hour.
