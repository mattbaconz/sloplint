One room. Two presses.

Offset Press started as a screen-print bench and grew a record label. Posters are pulled by hand, one color at a time, on uncoated stock. Records are cut, pressed and sleeved in small batches.

Artists keep their masters. When an edition sells out, the screens are reclaimed and the run is closed.
