Built for the one person holding the pager

Small teams don't need a monitoring suite. They need the right person woken up for the right reason, and nobody else.

Page on latency, not just downtime

An API that answers in six seconds is down to your customers. Set a threshold per endpoint, like p95 > 500ms for 2 checks, and Pingwell pages when it holds.

Checks every 15 seconds, from five regions

A slow route in one region isn't an incident. Pingwell confirms from a second region before it pages, so a bad network hop doesn't wake anyone up.

On-call rotations that match how you work

Build a weekly rotation in a minute. If the on-call engineer doesn't acknowledge in five minutes, the page moves to the next person.

Phone, SMS, and Slack, in the order you pick

Start with a push notification, escalate to a phone call. Every page shows the endpoint, the numbers, and a link to the last 60 minutes of checks.

Quiet when things recover

Pingwell closes the incident on its own when latency stays under the line for five minutes, and posts a summary of how long it lasted.
