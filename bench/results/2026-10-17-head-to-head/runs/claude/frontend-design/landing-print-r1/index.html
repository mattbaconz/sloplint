Ink first, then everything else

Offset Press is a three-person studio working out of a former sign shop. We pull every poster by hand on a four-post press, one colour at a time, and we cut our own stencils.

The label grew out of the print shop. Bands kept asking for posters, then for a record to go with them. Now we do both: we press short runs of vinyl for artists we’d buy anyway, and print the sleeves and inserts in the same room.

We keep editions small so each one stays worth owning. Nothing gets reprinted.
