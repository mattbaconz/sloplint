A print shop that also puts out records

Offset Press started with one press and a borrowed garage, printing gig posters for the bands we were in. Then the bands asked if we could do their sleeves. Then their records.

Everything we sell is printed one colour at a time on our own press, in water-based inks. Nothing is digitally reproduced, and every sheet comes out a little different. We think that is the point.
