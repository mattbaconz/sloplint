How preorders work

You choose what you want, we bake exactly that, and nothing waits on a shelf. Payment happens at pickup.

  1. Pick your bake

    Choose loaves and pastries from the weekend menu. Orders over $60 can be split across both days.

  2. Send the form by Thursday 8pm

    Pick a pickup day and a 30-minute window. We’ll email a confirmation within a few hours.

  3. We bake it Friday and Saturday

    Your order is tagged with your name and held on the pickup shelf.

  4. Collect and pay in person

    Show up in your window, pay by card or cash, and take everything home still warm.
