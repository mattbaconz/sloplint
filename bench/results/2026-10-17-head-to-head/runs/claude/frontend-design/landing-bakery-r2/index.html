How preorders work

No account and no payment online. You reserve, we bake, you pay at the counter.

  1. Choose your bread

    Pick what you want from the weekend menu and a pickup day.

  2. Send it by Thursday 8pm

    That's when we mix the dough. Later orders wait for the walk-in line.

  3. Get a confirmation

    We email within a few hours with your pickup window and total.

  4. Collect and pay

    Come to the side door. Your bag is labeled and still warm.
