Two inks, one bench

Founded
2019
Based in
Foundry Yard, Unit 4
Team
Four people, two presses

Offset Press began as a poster workshop and grew a record label when the bands we printed for kept asking who could press their records. We pull every sheet by hand, one colour at a time, so each print carries a small drift between its inks. We keep it. It is how you can tell a person made it.

Every release is a limited run: records pressed in batches of 300 or fewer, posters in editions of 40 to 75, numbered and signed. When a run sells out, it is gone. We do not reprint.
