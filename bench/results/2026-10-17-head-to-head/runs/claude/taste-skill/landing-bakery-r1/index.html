Bread by the week, three sizes

Skip the ordering. Pick a size and a loaf is set aside for you every Saturday. Pause or cancel any time before Thursday.

One loaf $9 a week

For one or two people who like toast. Your pick of any sourdough or rye, changed whenever you like.

  • 1 loaf, your choice each week
  • Free slicing
  • Skip a week with one tap

The pair $24 a week

For a household that eats bread daily. Two loaves and a little something for Sunday morning.

  • 2 loaves, any mix
  • 2 pastries of the week
  • Priority collection from 7 am

The full table $42 a week

For big families and shared flats. Enough bread for the week and a box of pastries for the weekend.

  • 3 loaves, any mix
  • 6 pastries of the week
  • A tub of cultured butter, monthly
