One press, one room, two inks at a time.

Offset Press is a small print studio that grew a record label. The people who pull the posters also cut the lacquers, design the sleeves, and pack the orders.

Screen printing lays one ink per screen. Where two inks overlap you get a third colour, and where the paper slips a hair you get an edge nobody drew. We keep that. It is how you tell a hand-pulled print from a copy.

We make each run once, count it out, and reclaim the screens. No restocks, no second pressing.

  1. SeparateArt is split into one flat layer per ink.
  2. BurnEach layer is exposed onto its own mesh screen.
  3. PullInk is dragged across the screen with a squeegee, one sheet at a time.
  4. NumberEvery sheet is checked, signed and counted. Then the screens are wiped.
