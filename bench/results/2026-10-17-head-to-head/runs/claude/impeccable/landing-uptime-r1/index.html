Built for the 3 a.m. message.

Up or down is the easy question. Pingwell pages on the harder one: is it fast enough?

  • A threshold on every endpoint

    Set p50, p95 or p99 per route. Your checkout can have a tighter line than your admin panel, and each one pages on its own terms.

  • Pages the person on call

    Follows your rotation. The alert goes to whoever holds the pager right now, by push, SMS or phone call, not to a channel everyone has muted.

  • Escalates if nobody answers

    Unacknowledged in five minutes? Next person. You choose the wait and the ladder, so a missed alert never ends in silence.

  • Waits for a real breach

    Require several slow checks in a row before anyone is woken. One hiccup from a cold start stays a hiccup.

  • Tells you when it's over

    A short all-clear goes out when latency settles back under the line, with how long it stayed over, so the morning review starts with facts.
