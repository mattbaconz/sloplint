One shop. Two ways to make a thing.

Offset Press is a small studio where the screen-printing bench and the record label share a room. The people who pull your poster also sleeve your record.

We started by printing gig posters for bands we liked, then started putting out their records. We work in short runs on purpose. A small edition means every sheet gets checked and every pressing gets played before it ships.

If you make music or pictures and want them made properly, in numbers you can actually sell through, write to us.

  1. SeparateArt is split into one flat layer per ink. No gradients, no tricks.
  2. PullEach colour is squeegeed by hand through its own screen, in register.
  3. PressRecords are cut, plated and pressed in short runs, then test-played.
  4. NumberPrints are signed and numbered. Records are hand-stamped. Then we stop.
