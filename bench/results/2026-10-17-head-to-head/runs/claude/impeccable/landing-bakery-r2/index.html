How preorders work

Four steps, no account, no app.

  1. Mon – Thu

    1

    Pick your bread

    Add loaves and pastries from the menu. The slip totals as you go.

  2. By Thu, 8 pm

    2

    Send the slip

    Choose Saturday or Sunday and a pickup window. You'll get a confirmation by email.

  3. Fri – Sat

    3

    We bake to count

    Dough is scaled to the orders we hold, so nothing sells out on you.

  4. Sat & Sun, 8–1

    4

    Walk in, walk out

    Say your name at the window. Your bag is waiting, and you pay at pickup.

Need to change something? Reply to your confirmation before Thursday 8 pm. Uncollected orders are donated at 1 pm.
