Everything between “getting slow” and a human awake

Up/down checks tell you it's already broken. Pingwell is built for the stretch before that, when a slow API is quietly costing you users.

  • every 30 s

    Checks that measure, not just ping

  • > 800 ms

    Your search route can be slower than your login route. Set a latency line for each one, and Pingwell only cares when that line is crossed.

    Confirmation before anyone is woken
